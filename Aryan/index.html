Our Services

Comprehensive Support Under One Roof

Every service is designed to build real-world skills and empower children toward greater independence.

🗣️

Speech & Language Therapy

Assessment and individualised therapy for communication, articulation, language and social interaction skills.

→
👥

Group Therapy: Literacy & Language

Interactive group sessions to build early literacy, language, social and classroom skills.

→
📖

Functional Literacy Programme

Practical reading, writing and communication skills for real-life independence.

→
🏠

ADL Training (Activities of Daily Living)

Building independence in daily routines like self-care, dressing, toileting, eating, and personal hygiene.

→
🤝

Social Communication Training

Developing social interaction, conversation, play skills, emotional understanding and pragmatic language.

→
👄

OPT (Oral Placement Therapy)

Structured approach to improve speech sound production, oral motor control and clarity of speech.

→
🎓

NIOS Academic Support & Reading-Writing Intervention

Individualised academic support, reading and writing intervention including NIOS curriculum support.

→
🏃

Physical Education & Motor Skills

Gross motor development, fitness, coordination and body awareness.

→
🔧

Pre-Vocational & Vocational Training

Life skills, work skills and vocational preparation for greater independence.
